0.10.2 Changelog

Twilight
 - Increased damage, fixed a bug that resulted in low damage
 - Alternate attack has more invul frames, aimable at the end, and gives opposite momentum before it ends to increase fluidity
 - Moved to Post-Mech (Added any mech boss soul as ingredient)

Paradise Lost
 - Increased damage, can be used in the air
 - Moved to Post-Mech (Requires hallowed bar)

Adoration
 - Charging now properly applies additional damage
 - Creates droplets as it travels, higher frequency when charged

Sound of a Star
 - Now a minion summon weapon
 - Active attack now changed to Right Click
 - Summons will focus enemies hit by the active attack

Pink
 - Removed innaccuracy while moving, damage now decreases instead
 - Inverted the fire rate, Shoots faster while moving and slower while not
 - Damage increased to mirror Terraria's Sniper Rifle (not the crit though)
 - Made the standing shots a little bit slower

Mimicry
 - Decreased Healing cap from 40 to 30
 - Increased damage ramp up from 300% to 450%
 - At max charge, damage ramp up is set to 600%

Smile
 - Increased Size
 - Removed the initial shotgun blast when swinging the weapon
 - Now creates a 3 chunks of corpses that is shot up in an arc on swing (similar to Red Mist)
 - Chunks rushes in to nearby enemies when it starts falling (has a low range)
 - Chunks does the shotgun blast the hammer does when it hits something
 - Right click does not affect player's velocity anymore
 - Right click's screaming shockwave now moves forwards instead of staying in place
 - Increased screaming shockwave damage since its not a multihit

Censored
 - Increased damage (well, more like reduced the damage reduction)
 - Right click does double the damage when hitting just as its about to reach its peak
 - Knockback calculations for grab was extremely wrong, knockback resistant enemies now cannot be grabbed

Da Capo
 - Increased damage slightly
 - Alt fire now has less damage reduction when piercing

Our Galaxy R
 - Properly reflects current description (accidentally replaced the text with planned reworks)
 - Requires players to have a team to apply healing

Red Mist
 - Increased Health and Defense
 - Defense increases by 5 for each player (up to 20 more defense)

Magic Bullet Bullet
 - The ammo bullet not the EGO, though I guess it also affects actual EGO bullet
 - Bullet now properly hits allies
 - Gains 5% damage multiplier for each ally alive (Maxes out at 50%)
 - If it hits an NPC or an ally player, gains 20% more damage
 - Fixed penetration damage fall off reducing damage by 2.5% instead of 7.5%

Certain effects now has slight bloom and can be turned off incase it breaks something screen rendering wise
