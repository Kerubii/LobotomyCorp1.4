Gold Rush
Road of the King - Alternate attack to target all 'Fragment of Bliss' and additional nearest enemies (Up to 5). 30 second cooldown
Intemperance - Critical hits apply a 'Fragment of Bliss', Only 5 enemies can have 'Fragment of Bliss'
Fragment of Bliss - +5 defense. Reduces 'Road of King' cooldown, heals 10 health and -10 defense when broken
Happiness - 5% increased damage for each 'Fragment of Bliss' broken with 'Road of the King'
!Voracity - When activating 'Road of King' under 50% health, Gain 'Greed' instead 'Happiness' for 1 minute
!Greed - This E.G.O gains 300% increased damage. 'Road of King' costs up to 50 health with no cooldown
